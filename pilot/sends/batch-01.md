## Before you send

1. **Find the human.** LinkedIn, the company's team page, or the posting's hiring manager. No
   `info@`, no `careers@`.
2. **Check for a warm path first.** One intro beats all ten of these. `INTERVIEW_OUTREACH.md` §A.
3. **Fill `contact_name`, `contact_role`, `channel` and `date_sent`** in
   `kill-test-tracker.csv` as each goes out. Nothing counts until `date_sent` is non-empty.
4. **Do not attach** the one-pager, the demo, or pricing. Not on the first send, not on the reply.
5. **Have the script open** for the call — `PREMISE_KILL_TEST.md`, Q1–Q11 in order, product not
   mentioned until Q9.

## What "done" looks like for this batch

Not "10 messages written" — that already happened. It's **10 rows with a non-empty `date_sent`**, and
then `python3 pilot/score_kill_test.py` still honestly reading `INCOMPLETE — 0 of 25` until the
first call actually happens.

yield 1–2 interviews.** That is the realistic expectation, and it is why batches 2 through 5 need to
