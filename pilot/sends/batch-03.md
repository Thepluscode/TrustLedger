# Batch 03 — sends 21–29 (exhausts the evidenced list)

> Rules per `batch-01.md` and `INTERVIEW_OUTREACH.md`.

**Read this batch honestly: it is the weakest of the three.** These are the remaining evidenced
rows after batches 1 and 2 took the sharpest. Seven of nine are brokerage — settlement pain is real
there but it is securities and client money, not multi-provider payouts. Two rows (CMC Markets,
Nuvei) are the weakest signals on the whole list and are included only because a send costs minutes.

**Send it anyway.** Nine sends for a few minutes' work is worth it at a 5–15% reply rate, and
sub-segment diversity is a gate requirement. But if the *only* pain that comes back is from this
batch, that is a finding about the wedge — it would mean the pain concentrates in brokerage
client-money rather than in marketplace/remittance payouts, and the ICP is aimed at the wrong place.
