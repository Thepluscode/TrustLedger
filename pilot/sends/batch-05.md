# Batch 05 — one-to-many, and the follow-up for batch 03

Two things that do not require a named recipient, which is why they close the gap the evidenced list
cannot.

---

## Part A — channel D: community posts

**One post reaches more qualifying people than the entire evidenced list.** A fintech operators
Slack has hundreds of exactly the right job titles, self-selected. It is the only channel here where
the reply rate is not bounded by how many names were sourced.

**Rules, or it backfires:**
- **Read the room's self-promotion policy first.** One post read as a pitch and the channel is
  closed permanently — a much larger cost than 29 cold emails.
- **Give before asking.** Offer the findings, publicly, back to the group. That is what makes it a
  contribution rather than a harvest.
- **Never name TrustLedger.** Same rule as every other channel.
- **One post per community.** Not a weekly bump.

### D1 — fintech / payment-ops Slack and Discord

> Doing research, not recruiting or selling.
>
> If you run finance or payment ops across 2+ providers: **what actually happens when a settlement
> doesn't match your records?** Who finds out, how, and how long after?
>
> I'm having ~25 conversations on this and will publish the anonymised findings back here — including
> the boring answer if it turns out most people have this handled.
>
> Reply or DM if you're up for 20 minutes.

### D2 — a founder / operator community (more context, less jargon)

> Research question for anyone running payments across more than one provider:
>
> When money goes missing between you and a provider — a short settlement, a duplicate, a payout
> that never lands — **how do you currently find out?**
>
> I'm trying to work out whether this is a genuinely expensive problem or one that a spreadsheet and
> a provider dashboard handle fine. Both answers are useful; I'd rather learn it's fine now than
> later.
>
> ~25 conversations, findings shared back here. 20 minutes if you're willing.

### D3 — LinkedIn post from your own profile

Reaches your warm network without asking anyone individually — and warm is the highest-converting
channel in `INTERVIEW_OUTREACH.md`.

> I'm doing research on a specific, unglamorous problem: **cross-provider payment reconciliation.**
>
> If your company settles across two or more payment providers, banks or rails, I'd like 20 minutes.
> Not a pitch — I'm testing whether the pain is real and expensive, or whether existing tools cover
> it. A well-argued "this is a non-problem" is as useful to me as the opposite.
>
> Speaking to ~25 companies. I'll publish what I find, anonymised.
>
> Comment or DM, and please tag anyone who lives this.

**Where to post:** fintech-operator Slacks, r/fintech, payment-ops Discords, Lithuanian/Baltic and
UK fintech communities (the evidenced list skews there — Vilnius, London, Brussels, Paris), Indie
Hackers, and your own LinkedIn. Post over several days, not all at once.

## Part B — follow-up wave for batch 03

Same templates and timing as `batch-04.md` — **F2 at +5 working days, F3 at +12, then stop.**

Batch 3 is the weakest tier, so expect the lowest reply rate. That is fine and does not mean the
follow-up was wasted: an explicit "we handle this fine" from a broker is a logged disconfirmer, and
disconfirmers are what the pre-registered STOP threshold is made of.

**One adjustment for Nuvei specifically** (batch 3 #29, flagged interview-for-insight-not-prospect):
do not use F3's exit framing. They are not a buyer, so there is nothing to exit from. If they do not
reply, drop it.

## The arithmetic, so the plan is checkable

| Batch 04, follow-up wave 1 (batches 1–2) | 20 | second touch |

| Batch 05A, community posts | 6–8 posts | one-to-many, reach ≫ 1 |
| **Total drafted** | **~85 individual sends + 6–8 posts** | |

`INTERVIEW_OUTREACH.md` estimates **~150–200 sends for 25 interviews**. At ~85 plus community reach,
this plan plausibly lands **12–18 interviews** — short of 25.

**The remaining gap closes with sourcing, not more drafting.** ~20 more evidenced companies (LinkedIn
Jobs behind auth, careers pages, FCA/EMI registers — listed in yield order in `TARGET_LIST.md`) would
add ~60 sends across three touches and clear the gate.

**Do not treat that as a reason to delay.** These 85 sends exist now and the first 29 can go out
today. Sourcing runs in parallel, and the first interviews will sharpen the ICP filter anyway — which
makes the *next* 20 names better than the 20 you would source today.

## Where this stops being drafting

Five batch files, ~85 sends, one instrument that scores them. Everything in `pilot/` is now written.

`python3 pilot/score_kill_test.py` reads **`INCOMPLETE — 0 of 25`** and will keep reading it through
every one of these files, because none of them is a conversation. The next thing that changes that
number is a reply, and the only input to a reply is a send.
