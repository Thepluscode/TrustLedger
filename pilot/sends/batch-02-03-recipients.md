- **Regulator registers (VARA/SCA/DFSA) confirm entities, not people** — good for "is this legal entity real and licensed", useless for finding the human. (DFSA Authorised Individuals, used in batch-01 for ThinkMarkets, is the exception.)
- **The vacancy pattern is evidence**: OKX and MultiBank are staffing treasury/reconciliation functions *right now*; Exness's posting names a reconciliation lead. The roles being created is pain-confirmation even where it removes the interviewee.
- **Gulf brokerages below C-suite are structurally invisible to public web** — Sales Navigator is the tool there, not more searching.
