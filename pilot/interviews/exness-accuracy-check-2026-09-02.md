## What this message evidences, read against the kill-test

The message is the founder's own account of what Artem said, so the following are quotations of
his summary rather than inferences drawn on top of it.

**Two statements point away from the premise:**

- *"you did not identify a specific gap in the current tools"* — the buyer, whose job title names
  reconciliation, did not report an unmet need.
- *"The organisation already invests engineering effort in improving the workflow"* — the
  substitute is in-house engineering, already funded.

**Three required bars are unmet in this conversation:**

| Bar | State |
|---|---|
| Pain — one named incident described unprompted | a *class* of incidents, no named instance |
| Exposure — quantified, even as a range | *"we did not quantify a specific example"* |
| Data — sample or integration access | *"Data cannot currently be made available"* |

Frequency, hands-on time and root cause were not established either, which is why six open
questions were asked.

**This is one conversation and not a verdict.** The kill-test needs 25. But it is a *disconfirming*
first data point, and the standing rule is that a negative must not be filed as neutral. If
interviews two and three return the same shape, the honest reading is that the premise is failing,
not that the questions need rewording.
