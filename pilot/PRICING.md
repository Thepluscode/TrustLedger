# Commercial Engagement Prices

These fixed prices come from the approved company rate card dated 2026-07-26, with TrustLedger
vertical pricing added 2026-08-01. Quote these numbers; do not substitute a range or negotiate the
same scope down. Pricing remains commercially unvalidated until a customer signs.

| Stage | Fixed price | Customer outcome and boundary |
|---|---:|---|
| **Payment Reliability Diagnostic** | **£1,500** | Entry point only when a buyer cannot yet share data. The fee is credited to Discovery booked within 30 days. A qualified buyer with a named incident enters at Discovery. |
| **Payment Reliability Discovery** | **£4,000** | Map one real workflow, data boundary, failure taxonomy, current exposure, integration plan and measurable pilot success criteria. Credited to a pilot booked within 30 days. |
| **Founding Design Partner Pilot** | **£10,000** | First three qualified customers only: one corridor, two data sources, six weeks, weekly feedback, controlled data access, a named operational sponsor and permission to use approved results. |
| **Standard Payment Reliability Pilot** | **£20,000** | Broader integration and security review, operational metrics and a value report. Exact scope is agreed after Discovery. |
| **Production deployment** | **Specific quote after Discovery** | Quote one exact figure for the evidenced scope; do not quote an annual range. Continuous operation starts only after the market and product gates pass. |

The £10,000 founding pilot is smaller scope plus participation obligations, not a discounted
£20,000 pilot. When the three founding slots are used, they are gone. Every pilot price buys evidence
and operational learning, not transaction initiation, routing, retry, reversal or remediation.
Customer-hosted processing or unusual integration requirements change scope, not the published
price of an unchanged package.

## Commercial gate

The current market gate requires at least two paid-discovery or paid-pilot commitments. A product
pilot is not considered passed merely because it ran: one paid pilot must be signed and every safety,
accuracy, recall, speed, evidence and adoption bar in `PILOT_CHECKLIST.md` must clear.

Do not quote a subscription yet. Subscription pricing is set only after three pilots have closed and
actual infrastructure and support cost per customer has been measured.
